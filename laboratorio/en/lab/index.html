SENTIDIÑO is a cultural citizenship and applied humanities lab connecting research, creation and place-based intervention.

01

What is SENTIDIÑO?

SENTIDIÑO is grounded in the conviction that the humanities can contribute directly to interpreting contemporary problems and transforming the contexts in which we live.

We work with specific places, communities and problems, combining humanistic research, cultural mediation, participatory methodologies and digital tools.

Our aim is to turn humanistic knowledge into forms of action: maps, archives, itineraries, prototypes, digital resources, mediation processes and new forms of cultural participation.

02

A Galician genealogy

SENTIDIÑO situates itself within a Galician tradition of cultural experimentation for which the Laboratorio de Formas, founded in 1963 by Luís Seoane and Isaac Díaz Pardo, provides a fundamental point of reference.

Its capacity to connect memory, cultural forms, place and intervention — and to turn ideas into projects and institutions — offers a genealogy from which to rethink the relationships between knowledge, culture and society today.

03

The humanities as a laboratory

SENTIDIÑO also draws inspiration from contemporary practices in the experimental humanities, and particularly from the work of the Experimental Humanities Lab.

We understand the laboratory as a way of doing research through making: asking questions, experimenting with methods, building prototypes, testing forms of mediation and circulating knowledge beyond exclusively academic formats.

The lab thus operates as an experimental space between university and society, where research also means making, testing, sharing and intervening.
