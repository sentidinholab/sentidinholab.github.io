SENTIDIÑO is a cultural citizenship and applied humanities lab connecting research, creation and place-based intervention.

01

What is SENTIDIÑO?

SENTIDIÑO is grounded in the conviction that the humanities can contribute directly to interpreting contemporary problems and transforming the contexts in which we live.

We work with specific places, communities and problems, combining humanistic research, cultural mediation, participatory methodologies and digital tools.

Our aim is to turn humanistic knowledge into forms of action: maps, archives, itineraries, prototypes, digital resources, mediation processes and new forms of cultural participation.

02

A Galician genealogy

SENTIDIÑO situates itself within a Galician tradition of cultural experimentation for which the Laboratorio de Formas, founded in 1963 by Luís Seoane and Isaac Díaz Pardo, provides a fundamental point of reference.

Its capacity to connect memory, cultural forms, place and intervention — and to turn ideas into projects and institutions — offers a genealogy from which to rethink the relationships between knowledge, culture and society today.

03

Laboratory work

At SENTIDIÑO, we understand the laboratory not simply as a space for research, but as a way of working. Laboratory work brings together reflection and practice, research and intervention, and understands knowledge as an open process constructed collectively.

Working in a laboratory mode means formulating questions and putting them to the test; combining methods and forms of knowledge; working with communities and places; building prototypes and devices; observing their effects; discussing the results; and, when necessary, revising the questions, methods and research processes themselves.

This way of working fosters collaboration across disciplines, forms of knowledge and social actors. It also entails a situated and reflexive practice: asking not only what we research, but also where we research from, with whom, through which instruments, and for what purposes.

The laboratory thus becomes a space for experimentation, learning and cooperation between university and society. Research also means making, testing, sharing, revising and intervening.

04

Thinking about laboratory work

The idea of the laboratory in the humanities and social sciences is open to different traditions, practices and formulations. These readings and experiences form part of the constellation through which SENTIDIÑO thinks about and continually reassesses its own laboratory practice.
